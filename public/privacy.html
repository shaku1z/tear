Who this is from

TEAR is made by its developer (GitHub: shaku1z), who is the person responsible for the data described here. You can reach the developer through https://github.com/shaku1z/tear/issues. That page is public, so do not put personal information in an issue.

This page covers the version of the game played at its own address. The copies of the game on CrazyGames and itch.io are separate builds. They send nothing to the services below, and CrazyGames keeps its own privacy terms for its site.

What the game collects

  • An anonymous sign-in. When the game starts it signs you in to Google Firebase without asking for a name or email. This gives your browser an anonymous account identifier.
  • Your cloud save. Your progress (profile statistics, unlocks, coins and your chosen display name) is stored under your anonymous account so it can follow you between sessions. Only your own account can read or change it.
  • Sign-in with Google (optional). If you choose to sign in with Google, Google handles that sign-in and keeps the account. The game receives your Google display name and does not store your email address.
  • Leaderboard entries and published replays (public). When you finish a run that earns a place, the game stores your leaderboard name, score, wave, time and your anonymous account identifier. A personal-best replay of the run, with a small preview image, can be published alongside it. Anyone can see leaderboard entries and replays.
  • Anonymous run statistics (optional). At the end of a run the game can send the mode, difficulty, wave, score, time and some economy figures, to help balance the game. This record carries no name and no account identifier. You can turn it off in Settings, under General, with "Share anonymous run statistics". When it is off, nothing is sent.
  • Game balance settings. The game fetches a few numbers (such as reward multipliers) from Firebase Remote Config. Google receives the request details any web request carries, such as your IP address.
  • Hosting logs. The site is served by Cloudflare, which records standard request information (such as IP address, browser and the page requested) to deliver the site and keep it secure.

The game also keeps your progress and settings in your own browser's storage on your device.

Why, and the legal basis

  • Cloud save, leaderboards and replays are the game's online features. The data above is needed to provide them.
  • Anonymous run statistics are used to balance the game. The legal basis is the developer's legitimate interest in improving the game, and you can switch them off at any time.
  • Sign-in with Google happens only when you choose it, which is your consent. You can sign out at any time.
  • Hosting logs are kept for delivering the site and its security, a legitimate interest.

Who receives the data

  • Google (Firebase) stores the cloud save, leaderboards, replays and run statistics, and runs the anonymous sign-in and Remote Config.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site and keeps the hosting logs.

Both companies operate in the United States and elsewhere, so your data can be transferred to and processed in the United States. Leaderboard entries and replays are public to everyone.

How long it is kept

  • Cloud saves, leaderboard entries and replays are kept until they are deleted at your request, or replaced when you beat your own best.
  • Run statistics carry no identifier, so they cannot be linked back to you. They are kept for as long as they are useful for balancing the game.
  • Cloudflare keeps hosting logs under its own retention rules.

Your rights

You can ask what the game holds about you, and ask for it to be deleted, by opening an issue at https://github.com/shaku1z/tear/issues. Say what you want (access or deletion) and give your leaderboard name, but nothing more personal. The developer will reply there about how to confirm that the data is yours. Run statistics cannot be found or deleted on request, because they carry no identifier. Depending on where you live you may also have the right to object to processing, to correct your data, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Turning off "Share anonymous run statistics" in Settings stops the statistics. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes what the game keeps on your device, and the game then starts with a new anonymous account.

Children

TEAR is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child's data has been stored, open an issue and it will be removed.

Changes

If what the game collects changes in a way that matters, this page changes with it, the effective date at the top is updated, and the game shows its first-run notice again.
