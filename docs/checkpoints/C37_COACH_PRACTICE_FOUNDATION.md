### Recorded Run DNA Theater panel

The normal Theater now has a `RUN DNA` control with a read-only capsule
projection. It calculates `run-dna-v1` only when the verified durable capsule
contains exactly one complete declared `run-dna-metrics-v1` result. The panel

and the five derived dimensions. Missing fields, no declaration, and duplicate

This is a single-run transparent projection, not a career archive, ranking,

ambiguous/absent refusal; the screen renderer contract retains the semantic

Fresh ordinary whole-run recordings now passively measure the eight inputs
through the actual shared combat owners. `native-combat-episodes-v1` is a new
capture policy, not a reinterpretation of historical `run-dna-v1` formulas or
receipts. Dangerous held swings, accepted throws and primary discharges start
episodes; actual damaging enemy contacts make one episode successful even for
multiple targets. Derived projectiles/AoE stay with their initiator. A completed
zero-contact episode is a miss; interrupted episodes are censored and make
precision unavailable. Zero accepted attacks also leaves precision unavailable.

Combat/moving ticks count executed eligible steps and actual player displacement
during locomotion, excluding relocation. Paused scheduler tails are recorded as
skipped-through coverage, never combat ticks or invented commits. Damage is
actual mitigated/clamped HP loss; healing does not subtract it. `maxHp` is the
peak effective value. The gameplay-owned maneuver catalog includes baseline
movement/strike/transport/parry families, weapon-specific mechanics, extra
airborne dash charges and Phase Step when actually enabled. Its denominator is
the union of legal capabilities; the numerator records accepted native uses.
Numeric buffs do not manufacture capability IDs.

The result binds its original recording session/run, committed endpoint,
scheduled frontier, terminal fact and coverage. Native termination inside a
multi-step application frame waits for that actual tick's observations and its
existing frame-end witness before seal. Fork/practice origins, observation gaps,
partial failed steps, unknown capture schemas and inconsistent identity/coverage
cannot become whole-run metrics. Old capsules without evidence remain
unavailable; existing no-capture legacy declarations retain their old semantics.
These are transparent recorded facts under verified capsule custody, not a new
metric re-execution, anti-cheat or skill/grade claim.

### Selected local run history (native observations v2)

Normal Theater Coach now lists only capsule metadata until the player explicitly
selects distinct compatible recordings. The open run plus one to seven selected
runs are verified sequentially in one cancellable module worker. Reselection,
closing Coach or leaving Theater terminates that worker; late results cannot
replace the current selection. This path writes no analysis, profile or career
records. Existing v1 analyzer/practice API bytes and hash semantics remain intact;
the normal UI no longer presents their fall/blade-event proxies as idle or misses.

The v2 comparison requires current complete event-bearing profiles and the same
build fingerprint, mode, difficulty, weapon, 120 Hz clock and replay-origin class.
It counts over the explicit shared verified checkpoint interval `(from,to]`,
with `to-from` recorded ticks as denominator. It displays the selected sample
count, min/median/max count distribution and chronological per-exact-measure run
history: identified throws, catches and resolutions, distinct deflected
projectiles, explicit perfect deflections, enemy defeats, waves cleared and void
rescues. Unknown identity/flags make affected measurements unavailable while
retaining known counts and contributing/unclassified event IDs. No guessed alias
correlation, damage/idle/accuracy/skill grade, improvement or human-play claim is
made. Imported/local residence does not establish ownership or authorship. Dates
are recorded metadata, ordered by parsed epoch; different seeds/loadout choices
remain descriptive differences rather than controlled causal comparisons.

The worker preflights at most eight sources, 128 MiB declared decoded data,
64 MiB compressed data and 4096 chunks before reading tracks. These are local
analysis policy bounds, not device memory/performance guarantees. It uses the
existing integrity reader and production replay admission; its verification
means compatible origin/checkpoint continuation, not re-execution of every
intervening causal event. Selected roots/manifests, version and shared interval
bind the output. Source identities are checked again before publishing and before
cited seek/practice. The first 256 target citations are navigable; all measurement
witness IDs remain in the immutable analysis result (details show bounded samples).

## Local recorded challenges

Theater now offers an explicit LOCAL CHALLENGE at a verified playable checkpoint.
Available rules are a native throw-and-catch return race when the source actually
contains one, clearing the captured current wave, and defeating a captured living
boss. A contact/throw-resolved event cannot satisfy return. Broader Daily Echo,
learned, Nemesis and TearBot authorities remain unavailable.

The child retains the actual absolute fork tick, released held input, captured
world/configuration/RNG and immutable parent roots. Its independent versioned
fork origin and fresh application-frame epoch do not reinterpret ordinary v1
tick-zero replay context. Parent absence/drift, unknown origins and pre-origin
seeks refuse. Play remains unranked with no saved progression or publication;
local recording is the explicit exception to ordinary practice's no-recording
policy. It uses the local recorder drain/seal path, never score submission or
publishing finish. Preparing work is cancelled before replacement can adopt or
resume a stale world.

Actual native goals stop the attempt at a committed step; Pause → MAIN MENU → QUIT or
replacement abandons it. A sealed attempt is reread and reexecuted from its
origin, without adopting later keyframes as proof of a goal. Only a reproduced
goal can succeed, and defeat needs a reexecuted native terminal event. The
separate retained result uses guarded parent/child manifest identities and is
rederived on reopen. Completing a bounded challenge does not claim game victory.
Endpoint snapshots are captured at the committed step before pause; drain/seal
waits for the real application frame to close. Theater ends at the last verified
authoritative receipt, retaining clock-tail evidence without simulating skipped ticks.

Focused production controls exercise a tick-120 fork, actual native catch,
abandoned recording, seal/reopen, exact result retry, foreign parent and
pre-origin refusal, false defeat refusal, source byte preservation and cancelled
preparation ownership. No human, learned-quality or population claim follows
from these engineered controls.
