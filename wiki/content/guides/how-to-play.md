TEAR: BLADE puts a spring-physics blade in your hand and sends you against waves of brightly coloured enemies. You are a black silhouette; your foes carry the colour, and an enemy's colour tells you its type, its affixes and its phase.

## The loop

Fight a wave, pick an upgrade, repeat; each stage ends in a boss. Beating a boss lets you raise one of your tiered abilities. See [Upgrades](/upgrades/), [Stages](/stages/) and [Modes](/modes/).

## Style

Cuts, throws, launches, slams, deflects and parries all feed the Style meter. Varied actions score more than repeating one, and taking a hit costs you meter. The exact points and ranks are on [Combat](/combat/).

## Reading the HUD

- **Health and shields.** Taking damage also costs Style, so staying healthy keeps your offense going.
- **Style rank.** Shows the current multiplier; it drains if you stop.
- **Wave, queue, coins and score.** Use the enemy queue to prepare for the next threat instead of reacting late.
- **Reticle.** Shows where the blade is aimed and whether it is held, thrown, stuck or returning.

HUD colours adapt to each biome so the important signals stay readable on light and dark arenas.

## Habits that keep you alive

- Read enemy tells instead of relying on passive safety.
- Make the blade's speed, angle and commitment count.
- Kill support enemies first: they multiply the danger of everything else.
