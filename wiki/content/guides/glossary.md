---
title: Glossary
description: Short definitions of the terms used across the wiki.
---

- **Affix**: a modifier rolled onto an individual enemy. See [Affixes](/enemies/affixes/).
- **Commitment**: extra damage from moving with your swing.
- **Coyote time / jump buffer**: input forgiveness around ledges and landings.
- **Deflect**: sending a projectile back with a fast swing.
- **Elite**: a rarer, stronger enemy roll. See [Affixes](/enemies/affixes/).
- **i-frames**: a short window where you can't be damaged, for example during a dash.
- **Parry**: a fast swing through an attack that negates it and opens a counter window.
- **Stage**: a biome of waves that ends in a boss.
- **Style**: the multiplier built from varied actions. See [Combat](/combat/).
- **Variant**: a named version of an enemy that appears from a given wave.
- **Wave**: one encounter within a stage.
