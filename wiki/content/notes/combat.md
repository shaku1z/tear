**The blade** is a spring, not a cursor. Wide, committed swings build tension and speed; weak flicks don't. Damage comes from blade speed at the moment of contact, so slow contact doesn't count as a hit, and one swing can hit several enemies.

**Commit and angle.** Move with the swing: your body's movement adds commitment, and a perpendicular cut is far stronger than a straight poke.

**Throws** inherit the blade's momentum, so a throw released from a fast swing hits harder.

**Parry and deflect.** Swing fast through an incoming shot to send it back; a well-timed fast swing also breaks melee pressure and opens a counter window.

**Launches and slams.** A fast upward swing pops enemies airborne; a downward hit while airborne slams them. Launching while rising (Updraft) and slamming during a fast fall (Power Slam) score more.

**Dash** is both an escape and a way to carry speed into the next swing: part of the dash speed is kept when it ends.
