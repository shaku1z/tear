**Adventure** is the campaign: each biome brings its own enemy pool and ends in a boss. Draft for the stage you are in, not only for an abstract endgame.

**Endless** is score-chasing survival. Long runs depend on scaling damage, consistent defense and keeping Style up; status pressure, parry control and reliable movement matter as much as raw swing damage.

**Gauntlet** combines the endless enemy curve with recurring campaign bosses. Budget the run around the next encounter and keep defensive tools for boss transitions.

**Playground** is a no-consequence test arena for builds and interactions. **Tutorial** walks through swings, launches, slams, throws, recall, deflects and parries.
